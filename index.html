Our story

Built by two hoteliers who were tired of hotels that felt like airports.

Aurora Stay opened in 2014 when Maren Voss and Daniel Cho, both longtime hospitality managers, decided the mid-size hotel category deserved better: the reliability travelers need for business, with the kind of attention you'd expect from a much smaller inn. No call-center check-in, no upsell scripts — just a front desk that's staffed by people who actually work here, day after day.

Today that means 68 rooms across four categories, an in-house kitchen that doesn't outsource breakfast, and free parking in a neighborhood where every other hotel charges for it.
